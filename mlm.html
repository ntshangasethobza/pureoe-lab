Programme rules to implement

1. Genuine sales onlyCommission should be based on bona fide product sales to customers, not recruitment, joining fees or purchases made solely to qualify.
2. Clear sales milestonesRank 1 target: R6,000 Level 1. Rank 2 progression target: R12,000 combined Level 1 + Level 2. Rank 3 adds Level 3 at 5%.
3. Reversals and audit trailRefunds, chargebacks and cancelled orders must reverse related commissions through an auditable ledger; no silent balance edits.
4. Qualification and activationThe previously discussed purchase/monthly R700 stock activation rule remains subject to legal and commercial review before it is enabled.
